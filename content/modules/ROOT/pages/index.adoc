= Leapp-O-Matic: Automated RHEL 10 Upgrades with AAP
:navtitle: Welcome

Welcome to the *Leapp-O-Matic* lab. In this hands-on workshop you will use Ansible Automation Platform (AAP) to drive automated, in-place upgrades from RHEL 9 to RHEL 10 with the `role-rhel10-upgrader` role and the LEAPP upgrade utility.

Rather than running upgrades blindly, Leapp-O-Matic treats each upgrade as a series of validated phases. You will see first-hand how the role's preflight checks stop unsafe upgrades *before* any destructive change happens — and how compatible systems upgrade cleanly, end to end.

== What This Lab Covers

* How the `role-rhel10-upgrader` role automates a RHEL 9 → RHEL 10 upgrade using LEAPP.
* How to configure Red Hat Subscription Manager (RHSM) credentials in AAP.
* How an AAP workflow orchestrates the upgrade across multiple nodes.
* Why a workflow targeting under-resourced or incompatible nodes fails safely at preflight.
* Why a workflow targeting compatible nodes upgrades successfully, including automatic RHEL 9.x version normalization.
* How to verify the upgrade and handle post-upgrade tasks such as configuration conflicts.

== Prerequisites

* Basic familiarity with Red Hat Enterprise Linux and the Linux command line.
* A general understanding of Ansible and Ansible Automation Platform concepts (inventories, credentials, job and workflow templates).
* A valid Red Hat account with subscription entitlements (used to register systems during the lab).
* Access to the AAP Controller and lab environment provided for this workshop — no local installation is required.
